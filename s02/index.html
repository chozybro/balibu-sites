Our story

Late nights in Ubud.

Balibu is a bar and kitchen on Jl. Sugriwa, a few minutes from the centre of Ubud. Breakfast coffee at eight, burgers at lunch, cocktails when the sun goes down.

Beef patties are smashed to order on a hot plate, the brioche comes from our own oven, the coffee is Kintamani arabica roasted in Bali. Simple things, done properly.
