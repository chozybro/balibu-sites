Anatomy of a good burger

Balibu Smash — 95K. Every layer, labelled.

Burgers smashed to order, Kintamani coffee and house cocktails. Ubud, opening 1 December.

See the menu Find us →

Jl. Sugriwa No. 60, Padangtegal Kelod, Ubud · Daily 08:00 – 23:00

  1. 01

    Brioche bun

    Baked in our own oven every day. Soft inside, a shine on top, toasted on the plate before it meets the beef.

  2. 02

    Cheddar

    Laid on the patties while they are still on the hot plate, so it melts into every edge.

  3. 03

    Double smashed beef

    Two beef patties, smashed to order. Pressed thin on a very hot plate for a dark, crisp crust.

  4. 04

    House sauce

    Our own recipe, with pickles for a sharp bite. Ask for extra; everyone does.

A bar and kitchen, drawn up properly.
