01 — Our story

A small kitchen on Jl. Sugriwa with a big grill, a good espresso machine and no rush at all.

We smash every patty to order on a hot plate, bake our brioche each morning and pour Kintamani arabica roasted here in Bali.

Come for breakfast after the rice-field walk, stay for happy hour, leave when the music says so. Good food, good mood.
